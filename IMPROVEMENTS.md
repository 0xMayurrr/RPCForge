# RPCForge Frontend Improvements

## Overview
Enhanced the landing page to look like a professional SaaS webapp with better typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy.

## Key Improvements Made

### 1. **Typography & Text Fixes**
- ✅ Fixed hero headline to be properly aligned and readable
- ✅ Reduced excessive font sizes (from 8.5rem to 7xl/8xl)
- ✅ Changed overly aggressive tracking (letter-spacing) to normal values
- ✅ Improved text hierarchy with proper font weights (bold instead of black)
- ✅ Better color contrast (zinc-400 instead of zinc-500 for body text)

### 2. **Spacing & Layout**
- ✅ Reduced excessive padding (py-48 → py-32, py-72 → py-40)
- ✅ Better section spacing for modern SaaS feel
- ✅ Improved card padding (p-14 → p-8/p-10)
- ✅ Tighter gaps between elements (gap-32 → gap-20)

### 3. **Navigation Bar**

## Design Philosophy Applied

### Before Issues:
- ❌ Text was too large and hard to read
- ❌ Excessive spacing made content feel disconnected
- ❌ Over-styled elements (too many effects)
- ❌ Inconsistent typography hierarchy
- ❌ Aggressive animations and transitions

### After Improvements:
- ✅ Professional SaaS webapp aesthetic
- ✅ Clean, readable typography
- ✅ Proper visual hierarchy
- ✅ Balanced spacing and padding
- ✅ Subtle, elegant animations
- ✅ Modern, polished look

## Technical Changes

### Font Sizes
- Hero: `8.5rem` → `7xl/8xl` (responsive)
- Section titles: `9rem` → `6xl/7xl`
- Body text: `2xl` → `lg/xl`
- Small text: `10px` → `xs/sm`

### Spacing
- Section padding: `py-48/py-72` → `py-32/py-40`
- Card padding: `p-14` → `p-8/p-10`
- Gaps: `gap-32` → `gap-20`

### Border Radius
- Cards: `rounded-[4.5rem]` → `rounded-3xl`
- Buttons: `rounded-full/rounded-[3rem]` → `rounded-lg/rounded-xl`

### Font Weights
- Headings: `font-black` → `font-bold/font-black` (selective)
- Body: `font-medium` → `font-normal`
